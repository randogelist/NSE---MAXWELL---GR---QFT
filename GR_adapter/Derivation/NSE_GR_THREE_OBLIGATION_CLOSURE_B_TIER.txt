U1.1  JOINT SMOOTHNESS AND ALL MIXED COMMUTATIONS

Fix t_* in I. Apply U1-ML09 and U1-N05 to obtain a globally smooth periodic
g with g(s)=f(s) as complete spatial slices on an open J containing t_*.
Set g_*(s,x)=g(t_*+s-1,x). This is globally smooth and periodic, so U1-N04
applies on s>=0; near s=1 its conclusion is ordinary joint smoothness.
Undo the translation. Since G acts on a complete spatial slice,
G(g(s))=G(f(s)) for every s in J, including all spatial points at once.
Thus H(t,x)=G(f(t))(x) is jointly smooth near every (t_*,x).

Periodicity of every mixed derivative of F follows by differentiating
F(t,x+e_j)=F(t,x) on the open slab. Each derivative is jointly smooth.
Apply U1-N03 to spatial derivatives and U1-N06 to time derivatives.
Induction on the length of an ordered coordinate-derivative word gives

  D^alpha H(t,x) = G((D^alpha F)(t,.))(x)                (U1.1.1)

for all alpha, t in I, x in V. Smoothness permits arbitrary coordinate
order. The same localization using U1-ME06 and U1-N07 gives

  d_t^n mean(f(t)) = integral_Q d_t^n F(t,y) dy.         (U1.1.2)

No derivative is moved through a conditionally convergent series.
Every derivative passage is supplied by the stated smooth endpoints.

U1.2  AN ORDER-INDEPENDENT TWO-DERIVATIVE ESTIMATE

Let g:V->R be smooth and periodic; lambda_k=4*pi^2*|k|_2^2.
For k!=0 and fixed x, write C_k(y)=cos(2*pi*k.(x-y)).
In each coordinate j, periodicity of g, D_j g, C_k and D_j C_k cancels
both endpoint terms in two integrations by parts over [0,1]. Fubini is
valid because the functions and derivatives are continuous on compact Q.
Hence

  integral_Q C_k Delta g = integral_Q (Delta C_k)g
                        = -lambda_k integral_Q C_k g. (U1.2.1)

Equivalently U1-ME02 twice, finite summation, and U1-ME01 give U1.2.1.
For the actual native summand, using m(k)=-lambda_k^(-1),

  m(k) integral_Q C_k g
       = lambda_k^(-2) integral_Q C_k Delta g,          (U1.2.2)
  |m(k) integral_Q C_k g|
       <= (16*pi^4)^(-1)|k|_2^(-4)||Delta g||_infty.   (U1.2.3)

The k=0 summand is exactly zero. The cube volume is 1. Define

  S4 = sum_{k != 0}|k|_2^(-4), C0=S4/(16*pi^4).

For N>=1, the sup-norm shell contains
  (2N+1)^3-(2N-1)^3 = 24N^2+2
lattice points; each satisfies |k|_2>=N. Therefore

  S4 <= sum_{N>=1}(24/N^2+2/N^4)
     <= 26 sum_{N>=1} N^(-2) <= 52,
  0 < C0 <= 13/(4*pi^4).                              (U1.2.4)

The final inequality follows from
sum_{N>=1}N^(-2) <= 1+integral_1^infinity r^(-2)dr=2.
Thus U1.2.3 is an absolutely summable majorant uniform in x, and

  ||Gg||_infty <= C0 ||Delta g||_infty.                (U1.2.5)

The constant C0 is independent of g and of every derivative order.
There is no sum/integral exchange in this derivation: integration by parts
is modewise, and only the resulting absolutely summable bounds are summed.
