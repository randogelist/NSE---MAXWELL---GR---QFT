I08. UNIQUENESS AMONG ALL ANALYTIC COMPETITORS.

composition multiplies coefficient norms by at most eta^j for eta<=1;

  time-derivative germs. On a common product neighborhood the mean-value
  theorem, applied along each time segment, gives

  PDE, pull back by S_eta, and apply the superposition evaluation formula:
