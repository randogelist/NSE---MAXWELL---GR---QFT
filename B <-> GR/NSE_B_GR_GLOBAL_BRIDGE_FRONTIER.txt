3. INITIAL-TIME ANALYTICITY OBSTRUCTION

For a canonical GR collar centered at t0 = 0, native reconstruction gives an
analytic boundary velocity near t = 0. Exact initial matching then requires
the spatial datum a to be real analytic. Data a does not impose this condition.

MATHEMATICAL_SCOPE_EXAMPLE (not a newly audited Lean theorem):
  a(x) = e_1 * sum_{k >= 1} exp(-sqrt(k)) * cos(2*pi*k*x_2).
  u(t,x) = e_1 * sum_{k >= 1}
    exp(-sqrt(k) - 4*pi^2*nu*k^2*t) * cos(2*pi*k*x_2), t >= 0.
  p = 0; nu > 0.

Every polynomially weighted Fourier series converges at t = 0; a and the
forward shear are C-infinity in their respective spatial/closed-forward
domains. The shear has zero divergence and zero convection and solves the
heat equation. The coefficients exp(-sqrt(k)) do not have exponential decay,
so a is not real analytic. An analytic GR realization centered at zero with
decoded initial velocity a cannot represent this datum.

CONSEQUENCE = a universal datumwise realization through analytic collars at
  t = 0 cannot implement the literal FeffermanB domain.
NO_CLAIM = a resolution of arbitrary-data global NSE existence.
