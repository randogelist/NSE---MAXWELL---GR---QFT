0. EXACT TARGET

  ANALYTICAL_OBLIGATION_FRONTIER
  := forall nu : Real,
       0 < nu ->

2. SOURCE SEMANTICS
-------------------

Fix nu>0, a, and s : NormalizedForwardTrajectory nu a. The existing theorem

  NormalizedForwardTrajectory.solution

supplies

  s.solution : NSSolution nu a s.totalVelocity s.totalPressure.

Thus u=s.totalVelocity and p=s.totalPressure are jointly C-infinity on the
closed forward domain, spatially unit-periodic, divergence-free, and satisfy

  partial_t u + (u dot grad)u + grad p = nu Delta u.

At each tau>0 the source is ordinary two-sided smooth on an open neighborhood
of tau. Negative-time totalization is irrelevant.


3. PERIODIC FOURIER-WIENER CARRIER
---------------------------------

Let xi_k=2*pi*k for k in Z^3. Define

  ||c||_(A_sigma)
    = sum_k exp(sigma|xi_k|)|c(k)|_2,

with complex Euclidean coefficient norm and Frobenius tensor norm.

3.1. Smooth slices belong to A_0.

Periodic C^4 integration by parts gives

  |uhat(t,k)|_2 <= C_t(1+|k|^2)^(-2).

The right side is summable over Z^3. On compact positive-time intervals,
joint smoothness gives a common summable majorant; hence t |-> uhat(t) is
continuous into A_0. The native endpoint

  uniform_datumFourierCoeff_moment_bound

supplies the corresponding uniform polynomial moment bounds.

3.2. Algebra estimate.

From |xi_k|<=|xi_l|+|xi_(k-l)|,

  ||c*d||_(A_sigma)
  <= ||c||_(A_sigma)||d||_(A_sigma),

therefore

  ||v tensor w||_(A_sigma)
  <= ||v||_(A_sigma)||w||_(A_sigma).

A_sigma is a weighted vector-valued ell^1 Banach space.

3.3. Leray and arbitrary mean.

For k!=0 set P(k)=I-k tensor k/|k|^2 and P(0)=I. Then

  ||P(k)c||_2<=||c||_2,

  |[P div F]hat(k)|_2<=|xi_k||Fhat(k)|_F.

The divergence multiplier vanishes at k=0. The nonlinear term has zero
constant mode and the heat multiplier preserves the constant mode. No
mean-zero restriction occurs.

The alternative literature reduction is the Galilean transform

  w(t,x)=u(t,x+m t)-m,

where m is the conserved spatial mean. The direct proof retains m internally
and does not require this reduction.


4. PROJECTED MODE EQUATION AND MILD IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------

The exact physical Fourier/Leray chain gives, for t>0,

  d/dt uhat(t,k)
    = -nu|xi_k|^2 uhat(t,k)
      -[P div(u tensor u)]hat(t,k).                    (4.1)

For every positive starting time t_* integration gives equality in A_0:

  uhat(t_*+t)
    = exp(-nu t|xi|^2)uhat(t_*)
      - integral_0^t exp(-nu(t-r)|xi|^2)
          [P div(u tensor u)]hat(t_*+r)dr.              (4.2)

Uniform Fourier moment bounds justify integration, summation interchange,
and the A_0 identity.


5. COMPLEX SECTOR ESTIMATES
---------------------------

Fix 0<theta<pi/2 and omega>0. Let

  S_(theta,T)={z in C:0<|z|<T, |arg z|<theta},
  sigma(z)=omega sqrt(nu Re z).

For the heat multiplier S(z)=exp(nu z Delta), completing the square gives

  ||S(z)b||_(A_(sigma(z)))
  <= L||b||_(A_0),

  L=exp(omega^2/4).                                    (5.1)

For 0<s<1, set b_0=nu(1-s)Re z and

d=sigma(z)-sigma(sz)=omega sqrt(nu Re z)(1-sqrt(s)).

The estimate

  y exp(-b_0y^2+dy)
  <= [e b_0]^(-1/2)exp(d^2/(2b_0)),

with d^2/(2b_0)<=omega^2/2, yields

  ||S((1-s)z)P div F||_(A_(sigma(z)))
  <= exp(omega^2/2)/sqrt(e nu(1-s)Re z)
       *||F||_(A_(sigma(sz))).                         (5.2)


6. COMPLETE HOLOMORPHIC SECTOR SPACE
------------------------------------

Let X consist of A_0-valued holomorphic maps on S_(theta,T), continuous at
z=0, with

  ||U||_X=sup_z||U(z)||_(A_(sigma(z)))<infinity.

For fixed b, impose U(0)=b. An X-Cauchy sequence converges uniformly into
A_0. Continuous coefficient evaluation gives coefficientwise convergence;
Fatou gives preservation and convergence of variable-weight bounds. Local
uniform limits preserve holomorphy. Hence the affine source space and each
closed norm ball are complete.

The variable-weight Fatou step is an application-specific derived edge.


7. DUHAMEL CONTRACTION
----------------------

Define

  B(U,V)(z)
    = -z integral_0^1
        S((1-s)z)P div(U(sz) tensor V(sz))ds.

Using (5.2), the algebra estimate, Re z>=|z|cos(theta), and
integral_0^1(1-s)^(-1/2)ds=2,

  ||B(U,V)||_X
  <= C sqrt(T/nu)||U||_X||V||_X,

  C=2exp(omega^2/2)/sqrt(e cos(theta)).                (7.1)

On compact subdisks the integrand and its z-derivative have integrable
(1-s)^(-1/2) majorants. The Mathlib parametric-integral derivative endpoint
therefore gives holomorphy of B.

Choose M>0 with ||b||_(A_0)<=M and set

  R=2LM,
  T=nu/(64C^2L^2M^2).

Then K=Csqrt(T/nu)=1/(8LM), KR=1/4, and 2KR=1/2. The map

  Phi(U)=S(.)b+B(U,U)

maps the closed radius-R ball into itself and satisfies

  ||Phi(U)-Phi(V)||_X<=(1/2)||U-V||_X.

Mathlib's

  ContractingWith.fixedPoint;
  ContractingWith.fixedPoint_isFixedPt;
  ContractingWith.fixedPoint_unique

produce the unique analytic mild fixed point U_*.


8. JOINT ANALYTICITY
--------------------

For any positive real z_0<T choose a complex disk D with closure inside the
sector. Choose

  0<sigma_*<inf_(z in D)sigma(z).

For |Im zeta|<sigma_* define

  U_tilde(z,zeta)
    = sum_(k in Z^3) Uhat_*(z,k)exp(i xi_k dot zeta).

The weighted bound gives locally uniform absolute convergence on compact
subsets. Every term is jointly holomorphic; therefore U_tilde is jointly
holomorphic in complex time and complex space. Its real restriction is
jointly real analytic.

Alternative native endpoint: Cauchy time estimates plus the positive spatial
exponential margin give

  ||D^n u||<=M_0 A_0^n n!.

Then

  NSEGR.Assembly.analyticAt_of_iteratedFDeriv_bound

proves the same joint real AnalyticAt conclusion.

9. IDENTIFICATION WITH THE GIVEN SMOOTH TRAJECTORY
--------------------------------------------------

The supplied smooth solution and U_* satisfy the same A_0 mild equation and
same initial value at t_*. For two bounded A_0 mild solutions U,V,

  ||U(t)-V(t)||_(A_0)
  <= C_mild M_0 integral_0^t
       (t-r)^(-1/2)||U(r)-V(r)||_(A_0)dr.

Choose h>0 with 2C_mild M_0 sqrt(h)<1. The supremum on [0,h] is zero.
Finite restarting yields equality on every compact common real interval.
Thus U_* is the supplied trajectory. No weak-solution uniqueness theorem is
used.


10. ARBITRARY POSITIVE CENTER
-----------------------------

Fix tau>0 and define

  M_tau=max(1,sup_(r in [tau/2,tau])||uhat(r)||_(A_0)),
  T_tau=nu/(64C^2L^2M_tau^2),
  h_tau=min(tau/4,T_tau/4),
  t_*=tau-h_tau.

Then t_*>=3tau/4>0, ||uhat(t_*)||_(A_0)<=M_tau, and 0<h_tau<T_tau.
Restart Sections 5--9 at t_*. The point h_tau is interior to the complex
sector. Hence u is jointly real analytic near (tau,x) for every x. Since tau
and x are arbitrary,

  AnalyticOnNhd Real
    (fun z : Real x Vec => u z.1 z.2)
    positiveSpacetime.

No radius uniform as tau downarrow zero is asserted. Initial-time
nonanalyticity remains admissible.
