1. DEPENDENCY DECOMPOSITION
---------------------------
E01: closed-slab jets + spatial shift invariance
  -> compact-cell bounds for u and its spatial derivatives.
E02: bounds for u and D_x u
  -> global spatial Lipschitz bound + whole-slab trajectories.
E03: trajectory uniqueness
  -> canonical eta=X(t;0,a), rho=X(0;t,x), inverse laws, eta(0)=id,
     and lattice equivariance.
E04 (PREFERRED CANDIDATE): first variation + parameter-linear Volterra
  differentiation on the closed slab -> continuous spatial/mixed jets
  -> actual joint within derivatives -> successor induction
  -> joint within-slab C-infinity eta. The joint within derivative bridge
  is an unproved native target; separate smoothness alone is insufficient.
E05 (ALTERNATIVE): finite-order reflection extension of u at 0,T
  -> finite-order open-domain version of E04 -> restrict and identify by
  uniqueness -> joint within-slab C-infinity eta.
E06: actual variational derivative F'=G F + polynomial determinant calculus
  -> J'=J trace(G)=0, J(0)=1 -> det F=1.
E07: forward within-slab C-infinity + E06 block invertibility + continuous
  set-theoretic inverse -> within-slab inverse derivative + smooth induction
  -> joint within-slab C-infinity rho.
  Alternative: E05 open-neighborhood flows + ordinary local inverse theorem.
E08: unique within-interval derivative
  -> actual materialVelocity equality -> MaterialPathOn package.

E04 is the principal missing analytic construction. E05 is a separate
nontrivial native seam-gluing proof for the open-extension route; a direct
within-slab forward-jet construction could avoid it. E07 has both a direct
within-slab inverse bootstrap and an open inverse-function route. Neither
route constructs the forward jointly smooth flow.
