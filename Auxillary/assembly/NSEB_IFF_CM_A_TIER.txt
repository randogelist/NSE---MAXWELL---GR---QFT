EXACT STATEMENT
---------------
forall nu : R, FeffermanB nu iff CMB nu.
Hence forall nu>0, FeffermanB nu iff CMB nu.

FeffermanB is the original NSEMaxwell.Classical.FeffermanB, unchanged.
CMB nu := forall a, Data a -> exists u p, CMSolution nu a u p.

CMSolution retains global jointly smooth velocity and pressure on t>=0,
spatial periodicity, divergence-free velocity, arbitrary velocity mean,
arbitrary periodic pressure gauge, and u(0)=a.
Its momentum condition is exactly:
forall T>0, exists eta rho,
  MaterialPathOn T eta rho
  and materialVelocity T eta = u composed with eta on [0,T]
  and MaterialEquationOn nu T eta (p composed with eta).

The same global u,p are chosen before the universal T quantifier.
No Eulerian NSE momentum equation occurs in CMSolution's hypotheses.
The literal deformation, inverse matrix, material acceleration, pressure
gradient and Piola diffusion expressions remain unchanged.
