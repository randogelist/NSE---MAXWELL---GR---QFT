-----------------------------

!=! [A_TIER]
NSEGR.Strengthening.NSE_GR_POSITIVE_TIME_BIUNIQUE_CORRESPONDENCE

For every nu>0, rho!=0, and datum a satisfying Data(a):

  (forall s : AnalyticForwardTrajectory nu a,
    exists! gamma : GRForwardFamily nu rho a,
      NSEGRCorresponds h_rho s gamma)

  and

  (forall gamma : GRForwardFamily nu rho a,
    exists! s : AnalyticForwardTrajectory nu a,
      NSEGRCorresponds h_rho s gamma).

The relation is physical:

  NSEGRCorresponds h_rho s gamma
    := forall tau>0,
         (nativeLocalEquiv nu rho tau h_rho).symm (gamma tau)
           = sourceGerm(s,tau).

It is not defined by an arbitrary carrier bijection.
