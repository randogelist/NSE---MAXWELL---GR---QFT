THEOREM:

  !  forall nu>0, forall c>0,
       FeffermanB(nu) -> MaxwellB(nu,c).

LOGICAL_SCOPE:
  The theorem transfers an assumed global NSE existence statement to the

1. EXACT OBJECTS
---------------

V=EuclideanSpace R (Fin 3), Q=[0,1]^3, S=[0,infinity) x V.
Volume is standard Euclidean Lebesgue measure, vol(Q)=1, and
<f>=integral_Q f. Spatial periodicity has period one in each coordinate.
Joint smoothness is exactly ContDiffOn R infinity on S. The time derivative
at zero is derivWithin on [0,infinity), not a two-sided derivative.

Data(a): a is smooth, spatially periodic, and div a=0.
MagneticData(B0): Data(B0) and <B0>=0.

The frozen inverse Laplacian is the actual cosine-integral operator

  Lf(x)=sum'_k lambda(k) integral_Q cos(2*pi*k.(x-y)) f(y) dy,
  lambda(0)=0,
  lambda(k)=1/(4*pi^2*|k|^2) for k!=0.

Scalar/vector instances are scalarL/vectorL, with the actual real Bochner
integral for the vector case. Define

  KB=curl(LB),
  U_m[B]=m+KB,
  G[B]=B cross U_m[B]+nu curl B,
  V[B]=-curl G[B],
  Q[B]=div G[B],
  DG[B](b)=b cross U_m[B]+B cross Kb+nu curl b,
  J[B]=c^2 curl B-DG[B](V[B]).

FeffermanB(nu) states that every Data(a) has a globally defined solution
(u,p), jointly smooth on S and periodic in space, satisfying

  div u=0,
  u_t+(u.grad)u+grad p=nu Delta u,
  u(0)=a.

The pressure p is spatially periodic. The mean of a is unrestricted.

MaxwellB(nu,c) states that every m in V and every MagneticData(B0) have
jointly smooth spatially periodic B,E,rho,J on S, with B(0)=B0, <B(t)>=0,
and

  div B=0,     B_t+curl E=0,
  div E=rho,   E_t-c^2 curl B=-J,

together with E=G[B], rho=Q[B], and J=J[B]. These constitutive equations
are part of the exact target.

2. SHARED FOURIER/HODGE RESULTS
------------------------------

Reference: ../FOURIER_HODGE_COMPOSITION_B_TIER.txt, C01-C12.

For every smooth periodic field f, the shared derivation establishes

  -Delta Lf=f-<f>,
  partial_j Lf=L(partial_j f),
  <Lf>=0,
  K(curl F)=F-<F>+grad L(div F).

The actual cosine definition is identified with its complex Fourier
multiplier by

  integral_Q cos(2*pi*k.(x-y)) f(y) dy
    = Re(exp(2*pi*i*k.x) fhat(k)).

Polynomial coefficient decay and rank-three lattice summability justify
every infinite sum and derivative interchange. The full integer lattice
contains both k and -k; no additional factor two enters the definition.

Consequences for admissible fields:

  U_<a>[curl a]=a,                                    (H1)
  curl(U_m[B])=B,  div(U_m[B])=0,  <U_m[B]>=m.        (H2)

The shared parameter theorem also establishes: if F is jointly smooth on
S and spatially periodic, then (t,x)->L(F(t,.))(x) is jointly smooth on S
and D_t L(F(t))=L(D_t F(t)). Hence

  D_t K(F(t))=K(D_t F(t)).                            (TC)

This follows from compact-time uniform Fourier bounds, interior series
differentiation, the Mathlib derivative-extension endpoint on the closure
of (0,infinity) x V, and finite-order smoothness induction. No analyticity,
negative-time extension, or uniform infinite-time estimate is assumed.

3. F01: RECONSTRUCT THE INITIAL VELOCITY
--------------------------------------

Assume FeffermanB(nu). Fix c>0, arbitrary m, and MagneticData(B0). Put

  a=U_m[B0]=m+KB0.

Spatial smoothness, periodicity, and H2 give

  Data(a),  curl a=B0,  <a>=m.

Apply the assumed FeffermanB proposition to a. Obtain an NSSolution (u,p)
with u(0)=a. The choice of a uses the independent mean m; no mean-zero
restriction is imposed on the reconstructed velocity datum.

4. F02: VORTICITY PATH AND INITIAL CONDITION
------------------------------------------

Set B(t)=curl(u(t)). Actual spatial differentiation preserves the joint
smoothness on S and spatial periodicity. Equality of mixed partials gives
div(curl u)=0. Integration of periodic coordinate derivatives gives
<curl u>=0. Thus B is a MagneticPath:

  B jointly smooth on S,
  B(t) periodic,
  div B(t)=0,
  <B(t)>=0.

At t=0,

  B(0)=curl(u(0))=curl a=B0.

All assertions include the time boundary because the inherited spatial
derivative fields are smooth within S.

5. F03: CONSERVATION OF THE HARMONIC VELOCITY MODE
------------------------------------------------

For each component i,

  integral_Q (u.grad)u_i
    =sum_j integral_Q [partial_j(u_j*u_i)-u_i*partial_j u_j]
    =-integral_Q u_i*div u
    =0.

Opposite boundary faces cancel in every total derivative. Likewise the
means of grad p and Delta u vanish, using periodicity of p and u. Joint
smoothness on compact time slabs justifies differentiation under the cube
integral, including the right derivative at zero. Integrating the NSE gives

  D_t<u(t)>=0.

The fundamental theorem on every compact interval [0,T] therefore yields

  <u(t)>=<u(0)>=<a>=m  for every t>=0.

Each u(t) satisfies Data, so H1 gives the actual reconstruction

  u(t)=U_m[curl u(t)]=U_m[B(t)].                       (R)

6. F04: FARADAY FROM THE NSE
---------------------------

Write e=|u|^2/2. The pointwise Lamb identity and curl-curl identity give

  (u.grad)u=B cross u+grad e,
  curl B=grad div u-Delta u=-Delta u.

Substitution in the NSE, followed by (R), gives

  u_t+G[B]+grad(p+e)=0.

Spatial curl commutes with the actual within time derivative; the input
mixed derivatives commute in the positive-time interior and extend
continuously to zero. Since curl grad=0,

  B_t+curl G[B]=0,
  B_t=V[B].                                          (F)

Set E(t)=G[B(t)]. Then (F) is exactly B_t+curl E=0.

7. F05: GAUSS AND AMPERE WITH THE FROZEN CURRENT
---------------------------------------------

Define

  rho(t)=Q[B(t)]=div G[B(t)],
  J(t)=c^2 curl B(t)-DG[B(t)](V[B(t)]).

The electric Gauss law div E=rho holds by definition. The magnetic Gauss
law was established in F02.

From (R) and (TC),

  u_t=K(B_t).

The product rule and commutation with spatial curl therefore give

  E_t
    =B_t cross u+B cross u_t+nu curl B_t
    =B_t cross U_m[B]+B cross K(B_t)+nu curl B_t
    =DG[B](B_t)
    =DG[B](V[B]),

using (F). Rearrangement yields

  E_t-c^2 curl B=-J.

Thus Ampere uses exactly the prescribed current; no independent source
field is inserted into the Maxwell existence statement.

8. F06: CANONICAL REGULARITY AND PERIODICITY
------------------------------------------

The parameter theorem gives smooth U_m[B] on S. Products and actual
spatial derivatives give smooth E=G[B], rho=div G[B], and V=-curl G[B].
Apply the same parameter theorem to the smooth periodic trajectory V;
then K(V) is smooth. The explicit formula

  DG[B](V)=V cross U_m[B]+B cross K(V)+nu curl V

shows joint smoothness of J. Integer Fourier characters make L and K
periodic in x, and products and spatial derivatives preserve periodicity.
All required fields are therefore smooth and periodic through t=0.

This regularity calculation uses neither a global uniform time bound nor
analyticity. It is reconstruction of a supplied NSE solution, not a new
global evolution argument.

9. F07: EXISTENTIAL AND UNIVERSAL ASSEMBLY
----------------------------------------

For the arbitrary pair (m,B0), the fields constructed above satisfy every
component of MaxwellSolution(nu,c,m,B0): regularity, periodicity, magnetic
mean zero, both Gauss laws, Faraday, Ampere, all constitutive identities,
and B(0)=B0. Hence MaxwellB(nu,c).

Since nu>0 and c>0 were arbitrary,

  !  forall nu>0, forall c>0,
       FeffermanB(nu) -> MaxwellB(nu,c).
